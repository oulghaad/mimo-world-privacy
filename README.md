# Privacy Policy — Mimo World

**Application Name:** Mimo World: Family Playhouse (`com.mimoworld.game`)  
**Effective Date:** November 9, 2026  
**Target Audience:** Children (Ages 4–9) & Families

---

## 1. Overview & Commitment to Children's Privacy

**Mimo World** is an interactive 2D digital dollhouse and life simulation game designed for children and families. We are committed to protecting the privacy of children and complying strictly with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**, the **EU General Data Protection Regulation for Children (GDPR-K)**, the **Google Play Families Policy**, and the **Apple App Store Kids Category** guidelines.

We **do not** knowingly collect, store, use, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) from children or any other users of the application.

---

## 2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Mimo World does **not** require user registration or accounts, and does **not** request, collect, or access:
- Names, usernames, email addresses, physical addresses, or phone numbers
- Photos, videos, camera access, or microphone recordings
- Precise or coarse geographical location data (GPS)
- Contacts, calendars, or files stored on the device
- Persistent advertising identifiers (`AD_ID` / AAID on Android and IDFA on iOS are explicitly disabled and never collected)

---

## 3. Strictly Local Game Save Data

All in-game progress—including customized characters, selected outfits, furniture states, object placements, audio preferences, and language selection (English / French)—is saved **exclusively locally on the user's device** using standard local storage (`localStorage`).
- This data never leaves the device and is never transmitted to any external server.
- Parents or users can reset all local game data at any time from the in-app **Parental Space** or by uninstalling the application.

---

## 4. Child-Safe Advertising (Google AdMob)

Mimo World displays limited, family-safe advertisements (a banner on the World Map and a full-screen interstitial during screen transitions) powered by **Google AdMob**, a Google Play Families certified SDK.

To ensure 100% compliance with child protection laws:
- **Child-Directed Treatment:** Every ad request is locked in code with `tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true` (`TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT_TRUE`) and `tagForUnderAgeOfConsent = true`.
- **Content Rating G:** All ads are restricted to `maxAdContentRating = "G"` (`MAX_AD_CONTENT_RATING_G`), suitable for general and young audiences.
- **Non-Personalized Ads Only (`npa = 1`):** Interest-based advertising, behavioral profiling, and remarketing are strictly disabled.
- **No Advertising ID Collection:** The `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` permission is explicitly removed from the Android manifest (`tools:node="remove"`), ensuring no advertising identifier is accessed.
- **Limited Technical Diagnostics:** The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process temporary, non-personal technical data (such as ephemeral IP address, device model, and ad impression/click events over encrypted HTTPS) solely for contextual ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 5. Parental Gate & Adult Area

Any settings, compliance disclosures, or adult-oriented actions inside Mimo World are placed behind a dual **Parental Gate** (requiring the user to solve a multiplication/addition challenge and press-and-hold a confirmation button) to prevent accidental access by young children.

---

## 6. Third-Party Links & In-App Purchases

- Mimo World contains **no** unguarded external web links, social media integrations, or open chat features accessible to children.
- Mimo World contains **no** manipulative loot boxes or deceptive in-app purchases.
